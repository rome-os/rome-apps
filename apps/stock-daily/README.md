# Stock Daily

Stock Daily automatically generates an English US stock market close recap after the market session, explaining the day's moves, key themes, and watch items.

## What you get

- Scheduled English US market close reports for each trading day
- Clear summaries of index action, sector rotation, leaders, and laggards
- Key takeaways from earnings, macro events, and market sentiment
- Manual report generation whenever you want an update
- Archived report history for reviewing market context over time

## When to use it

Use this app if you follow US equities and want a structured daily recap without manually scanning many market sources. It is suitable for individual investors, US equity researchers, and anyone who wants to maintain a steady read on market conditions.
