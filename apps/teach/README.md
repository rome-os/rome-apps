# Teach

Your personal tutor and learning workspace — learn things that stick.

## What it does

Teach turns "I want to learn X" into a real, structured learning loop. You tell
it what you want to learn and why, and it generates bite-sized lessons tailored
to your level, turns them into review cards, and uses spaced repetition so the
knowledge actually sticks. It nudges you when reviews are due, so you keep
momentum without having to remember to come back.

## Features

- **Missions** — capture what you're learning and the deeper reason behind it,
  then track your progress over time.
- **AI-generated lessons** — get self-contained, readable lessons calibrated to
  your level, with current topics backed by cited sources.
- **Spaced-repetition review** — every lesson becomes flip-cards you grade
  Again / Hard / Good / Easy; Teach schedules each card for the right day.
- **Progress and streaks** — see lessons completed, cards due, and your review
  streak at a glance.
- **Proactive nudges** — get a reminder on your preferred channel when reviews
  are due (you choose when to switch this on).
- **A tutor you can talk to** — chat with the tutor to set up missions, get
  assessed, learn interactively, and quiz yourself.

## When to use it

Reach for Teach when you want to genuinely learn and retain something — a new
programming concept, a language, a domain you keep meaning to understand — and
you want lessons plus a review habit instead of scattered notes you never revisit.
