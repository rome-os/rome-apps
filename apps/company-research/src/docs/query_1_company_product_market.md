Write a tight research report on {COMPANY_NAME} ({COMPANY_DOMAIN}) covering three areas: company profile, product, and market context.

Use only public sources. Include grounding and citation links for every claim.

Start with a brief **Overview** section highlighting the most important findings — the key facts a reader needs to know at a glance before diving into details.

Company profile:
- Legal and common name, founding date, HQ location
- Primary geographies of operation
- Business model type (SaaS, marketplace, services, etc.)
- Industry tags and current company stage (seed, growth, public, etc.)
- for-profit or non-profit

Product and ICP:
- Primary product name and category
- Target customer / ideal customer profile
- Current customers or customer count/range
- Active users or active accounts
- Deployment or sales model (self-serve, sales-led, hybrid)
- Product maturity stage
- Value proposition — what specific problem it solves and how

Market context:
- Customer segments served
- Market category and category maturity
- Direct competitors with brief rationale for each
- TAM estimate; if no credible public figure exists, derive a directional estimate from available evidence and flag it as approximate

Rules:
- Do not guess. If a fact is not supported by a source, say so explicitly rather than filling in a plausible answer.
- Separate sourced facts from your own reasoning.
- Keep the report concise — aim for clear, information-dense paragraphs, not exhaustive prose.
- For each cited company, link the company's official website.
- If a source appears to refer to a different company with a similar name, ignore it.

Tone & Voice:
- Objective and analytical — avoid promotional or emotional language
- Use bold only for critical information
- Use third person ("the company," "management") rather than second person
- Be direct: state the view clearly and early, then support it
- Use "we" when referring to the author of the research report
