You are a growth marketing strategist. Based on everything you know about {COMPANY_NAME} ({COMPANY_DOMAIN}) — its product, positioning, target audience, and competitive landscape — produce a practical marketing strategy covering the following sections:

Use only public sources. Include grounding and citation links for every factual claim.

1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Define a primary and secondary ICP with specifics: role, pain point, behavior, team size
- List concrete signals that indicate a prospect is a good fit: tools they use, communities they frequent, behaviors they exhibit

2. Positioning Statement
- Write a positioning statement in the format: For [target] who [pain], [company] is a [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternatives], [company] [differentiator].
- Ground the differentiator in something real and defensible about the product

3. Messaging Framework
- Provide a table with: Headline, Sub-headline, Value props (3-4 bullets), Proof points, and Objection handling
- Match the tone to the brand voice visible on the company's public website and materials

4. Channel Prioritization
- Rank the top 5-6 marketing channels in order of expected impact: paid search, organic/SEO, social media, creator/influencer programs, events, partnerships, community
- For each channel, state the rationale tied to the ICP and product, not generic marketing advice
- Be opinionated. Deprioritize channels that do not fit and say why

5. 30-Day Roadmap
- Break into Week 1 through Week 4 with 3-5 concrete action items per week
- Action items should be specific and achievable by a solo founder or small team
- End with a clear success metric for Month 1

Rules:
- Be specific to the company's actual product and market, not generic.
- Reference real features, pages, or content from the company where relevant.
- When public evidence is limited, say so clearly and keep recommendations bounded by what is observable.
- Prioritize free and organic channels over paid for early-stage companies unless public evidence suggests otherwise.
- Keep recommendations actionable. Every item should be something the company could plausibly execute this month.
- Use markdown formatting with clear section headers.
