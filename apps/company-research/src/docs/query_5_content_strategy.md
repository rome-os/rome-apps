## 3. Sample Content

For each platform, write **two ready-to-publish sample posts** that the companycould use. Follow these constraints:

### X (Twitter)
- One single tweet (under 280 characters)
- One thread (3-5 tweets) that tells a story or teaches something

### LinkedIn
- One short-form post (under 1300 characters) optimized for the feed algorithm
- One long-form post (600-800 words) suitable for thought leadership

### Reddit
- One post for a relevant subreddit: identify the subreddit by name, write the title and body. Follow the subreddit's norms — do not be promotional
- One comment reply to a hypothetical question where the company's product is a natural answer. Be helpful first, product mention second

### Hacker News
- One "Show HN" post: title and body text. Follow HN conventions — be technical, concise, and honest about trade-offs
- One comment on a hypothetical relevant discussion thread. Provide genuine insight, not a sales pitch

### Official Blog
- One blog post outline (title, subtitle, 5-7 section headers with 1-2 sentence descriptions of each section, target word count, target keyword for SEO)
- One complete short-form blog post (400-600 words) — a product insight, market take, or customer story angle

- Be specific to the company's actual product, features, and market. Do not produce generic content that could apply to any company.

- Reddit and Hacker News content must be genuinely useful and community-appropriate. Overtly promotional content gets downvoted — write accordingly.
- Sample content should be ready to publish with minimal editing, not placeholder text.
