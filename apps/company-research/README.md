# Company Research

Build a structured profile of any company in minutes. Point it at a name or domain, and Company Research pulls together the people, funding history, products, metrics, and recent events into one searchable view.

## What you get

- One-click research that turns a company name into a structured profile
- People and roles — founders, executives, and key hires with their backgrounds
- Funding rounds, investors, and notable financial events
- Products, attributes, social pages, and active job posts
- Related companies, partnerships, and competitive relationships
- A company registry you can search and filter as your library grows
- Full source provenance on every field, so you can trace where each fact came from

## When to use it

Reach for Company Research when you're doing due diligence, building a target list, prepping for a sales call, or tracking competitors. Instead of a dozen browser tabs, you end up with a clean, comparable profile you can come back to later.
