# X Manager

Run your X (Twitter) account on autopilot — without losing your voice. X Manager learns how you write, helps you post, drafts replies to incoming comments, and keeps an eye on how your tweets are performing.

## What you get

- Learns your brand voice from your own past tweets, so new content sounds like you
- Drafts and posts tweets and threads, with optional images
- Reply drafts for incoming comments, ready for you to approve or tweak
- Scheduled metrics polling — views, likes, retweets, replies — so you know what's working
- A notifications view that surfaces what actually needs your attention
- Works through your real logged-in X account, so no API keys or extra setup

## When to use it

Reach for X Manager if you're growing a personal brand or a company account and want help showing up consistently. Great for founders, creators, and marketers who want to stay active on X without it eating their day.
