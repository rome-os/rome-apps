# YouTube Distill

Turn any YouTube video into a mind map, a key-point summary, and a web slide deck — in the video's own language.

## What it does

Paste a YouTube link and YouTube Distill pulls the video's transcript and turns
it into the study aids you actually want: an interactive mind map, a tidy
key-point summary, and a self-contained slide deck you can present in the
browser. It detects the transcript's language and writes every artifact in that
same language, so a Japanese talk stays in Japanese and a Spanish lecture stays
in Spanish. Every video you distill is saved to a browsable history.

## Features

- **Pick what you need** — generate a mind map, a summary, a slide deck, or any
  combination. All three are on by default.
- **Interactive mind map** — zoom, pan, and collapse a hierarchical map of the
  video's ideas.
- **Clean summary** — a short overview plus the key points, each with a one-line
  explanation.
- **Web slides** — a ready-to-present slide deck you can step through with the
  arrow keys.
- **Original language** — outputs follow the language spoken in the video.
- **History** — revisit, reopen, or delete any past video from one place.

## When to use it

Reach for YouTube Distill when you want the substance of a long talk, lecture,
tutorial, or interview without watching the whole thing — to review before a
meeting, take notes on a course, or turn a video into slides you can share.
