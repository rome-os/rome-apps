# News

Your morning paper, delivered. News pulls together top stories and Reddit chatter and sends you a single daily digest on Telegram — so you start the day informed without doom-scrolling.

## What you get

- A daily morning news digest delivered to you on Telegram
- Top headlines pulled from across the web
- Reddit signal layered in — what's trending in the communities you care about
- On-demand Reddit search and browsing when you want to dig deeper
- Scheduled Reddit scans on the topics you tell it to watch

## When to use it

Set this up if you want one quiet daily catch-up instead of a phone full of news apps and notifications. Great for staying on top of an industry, a niche subreddit, or just the day's headlines without falling into the feed.
