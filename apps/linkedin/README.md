# LinkedIn

Use LinkedIn through Rome with your real, logged-in account — read profiles, search people and jobs, message contacts, and send connection requests without leaving your workflow.

## What you get

- Look up any person or company profile, with their recent posts and open roles
- Search for people by keyword and location, and pull similar-profile suggestions
- Search and read job postings, including full job details
- Read your inbox, open a specific conversation, and search past messages
- Send messages with an explicit confirmation step, so nothing goes out by accident
- Send connection requests — or accept incoming ones

## When to use it

Reach for the LinkedIn app when you're sourcing candidates, doing sales outreach, researching a company, tracking a job hunt, or letting an agent handle the busywork of inbox triage and follow-ups. You stay in control of every message that gets sent.
