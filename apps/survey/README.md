# AI Survey

Turn a research goal into a real, conversational survey — without dragging question blocks around a builder. Tell the AI Survey app what you want to learn, and it designs a survey that feels like a chat, then talks to each respondent for you.

## What you get

- Describe your research goal in plain language and get a draft survey back
- Iterate on the survey by chatting with a design assistant — add, cut, or rephrase questions on the fly
- A conversational survey experience for respondents, with rich interactive elements instead of a static form
- Open-ended answers explored more deeply, the way a human interviewer would follow up
- Structured, analyzable results — even though the survey feels like a conversation
- A shareable link respondents can open without an account

## When to use it

Reach for AI Survey when you want richer answers than a typical form gives you — product research, customer discovery, NPS with real "why" follow-ups, candidate screening, or any time you'd rather have a thoughtful chat than push a generic questionnaire.
