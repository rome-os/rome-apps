# Code of Conduct

Rome Apps is built by people with different backgrounds, perspectives, and levels of experience. We want participation here to be useful, respectful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while still discussing technical disagreements directly.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions and help contributors understand repository conventions.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and security boundaries.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and move on constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, sustained disruption, or knowingly misleading claims.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a Rome OS repository maintainer privately or a moderator in the [Rome Discord community](https://discord.gg/g7EFmEtmqc). Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose sensitive personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is harmful to the project or community. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with attention to the safety of the people involved.

This policy applies in repository spaces and in other public spaces when someone is representing the project.
